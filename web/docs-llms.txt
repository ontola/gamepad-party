# Splitscreen Club developer docs

> Splitscreen Club puts local multiplayer games in one room: players pick a game, keep their controller and profile, and go back to the room with Back. These docs show how to connect a game made in any engine and share it through the platform. Start with Share your game.

All pages in one file: https://splitscreen.club/docs/llms-full.txt
